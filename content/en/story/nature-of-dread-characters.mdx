## The Protagonist: Hellhole's Unwilling Guest

The only character the store page describes in any detail is you. The framing is deliberate: you are "caught in the wrong place at the wrong time" and awaken inside a classified settlement. You are not a chosen one, not a soldier dispatched on a mission — you are a civilian-shaped problem dropped into a centuries-old conflict.

That setup tells us a lot about how the Nature of Dread characters will likely be structured:

- **You are an outsider.** Everyone else in Hellhole has history with the place. You do not, which makes you a natural audience surrogate for exposition.
- **You have something to protect.** The page explicitly asks how far you will go "to protect what truly matters to you," implying a personal stake outside the settlement.
- **You are not pre-equipped.** The weaponry system emphasizes scavenged gear — a jury-rigged sawed-off shotgun versus a standard-issue military pistol — which fits a protagonist with no military backing.

If the writing follows through on that premise, the protagonist's arc will be about a ordinary person being slowly forced into extraordinary cruelty. That is a very different character study than the power fantasy of a trained operative, and it shapes every interaction you will have with the rest of the cast.

If it holds up, the Nature of Dread characters on the monster side will feel less like obstacles and more like opponents with opinions about you.

The middle tier — the Horsemen-adjacent beings — is the biggest open question. Are they bosses? Patron figures? Something you bargain with? The teaser material does not say, and the community has nothing concrete to go on yet.

| Character or creature trait | Mechanical consequence for the player |

That last row is worth emphasizing. In a game tagged Story Rich, characters are also your primary source of information.
